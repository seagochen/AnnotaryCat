# AnnotaryCat 支持 / Support

## 中文

**报告问题或提建议：** 在 <https://github.com/seagochen/AnnotaryCat-Feedback/issues> 新建问题。应用里的「帮助 › 发送问题报告…」会帮你打开这个页面，并生成一份诊断包供你检查后附上。诊断包只含版本、系统信息和日志（日志只记录命令名、错误代码和耗时，不含项目数据、文件内容或密码）。附上后，问题和附件对所有人公开。

**订阅：**
- 订阅、续订和取消都在商店里处理：Mac App Store 在「App Store › 账户 › 订阅」，Microsoft Store 在 Microsoft 账户的「服务和订阅」。
- 换了电脑或重装后，在应用的「帮助 › AnnotaryCat Pro…」中点「恢复购买」。
- 退款请向 Apple 或微软申请。

**常见问题：**
- *数据存在哪里？* 在你选择的工作区文件夹里，应用不会上传任何数据。
- *Mac App Store 版为什么不能直接启动训练？* 商店版运行在系统沙盒中，不能启动应用之外的程序。AnnotaryCat 会检查数据集、写好训练配置和脚本，并给出命令，请在「终端」里运行。
- *Mac App Store 版重新安装后要再选一次工作区？* 沙盒授权与应用的签名绑定，重新安装或更换版本后可能需要重新选择工作区和数据集文件夹。
- *订阅结束后数据还在吗？* 在。需要 Pro 的项目仍可浏览和导出，只是在重新订阅前不能修改。

## English

**Report a problem or suggest a feature:** open an issue at <https://github.com/seagochen/AnnotaryCat-Feedback/issues>. In the app, Help › Send a problem report… opens that page and makes a diagnostics bundle for you to review and attach. The bundle holds only the version, system information and logs (which record command names, error codes and durations, never project data, file contents or passwords). Once attached, the issue and the bundle are public.

**Subscriptions:**
- Subscribing, renewal and cancellation are handled by the store: in the Mac App Store under App Store › Account › Subscriptions, and in the Microsoft Store under "Services & subscriptions" in your Microsoft account.
- On a new computer or after reinstalling, choose Restore purchases in Help › AnnotaryCat Pro….
- For refunds, ask Apple or Microsoft.

**Common questions:**
- *Where is my data?* In the workspace folder you choose. The app uploads nothing.
- *Why can't the Mac App Store version start training itself?* The store version runs in the system sandbox and cannot start programs outside the app. AnnotaryCat checks the dataset, writes the training configuration and a script, and gives you the command to run in Terminal.
- *Why does the Mac App Store version ask for the workspace again after a reinstall?* Sandbox permissions are tied to the app's signature; after a reinstall or a new version you may need to choose the workspace and dataset folders again.
- *Is my data still there when a subscription ends?* Yes. Projects that need Pro can still be browsed and exported, but not changed until you subscribe again.
