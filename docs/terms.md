### 1. 免费版与 Pro

AnnotaryCat 可以免费使用。免费版包含本地数据上的目标检测、图片分割和图片分类，项目数量不限。AnnotaryCat Pro 是自动续订的订阅，开通后解锁全部功能：其余任务类型、文本文档分类、SSH 远程数据集、控制通道与 MCP、自定义字段，以及以后推出的 AI 辅助功能。各功能以应用内「AnnotaryCat Pro」页面的说明为准。

- 除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，订阅会按相同周期和当时的价格自动续订；续订费用在周期结束前 24 小时内收取。
- 你可以随时在商店的账户设置中管理或取消订阅：Mac App Store 在「App Store › 账户 › 订阅」，Microsoft Store 在 Microsoft 账户的「服务和订阅」。取消后，当前周期结束前 Pro 仍然有效。
- 退款按商店的退款政策处理，请向 Apple 或微软申请。

### 4. 订阅结束后

订阅结束后回到免费版。你的数据不会被删除：需要 Pro 的项目仍可打开、浏览和导出，只是在重新订阅前不能继续修改。

AnnotaryCat 在你的电脑上运行，我们不收集、不上传你的数据，详见隐私政策。你对自己标注和处理的数据负责，包括取得必要的授权。

## English

### 1. Free plan and Pro

AnnotaryCat is free to use. The free plan covers object detection, image segmentation and image classification on local data, with no limit on projects. AnnotaryCat Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks everything else: the other task types, classifying text documents, datasets on SSH servers, the control channel and MCP, custom fields, and AI-assisted features as they arrive. The "AnnotaryCat Pro" page in the app describes what is included.

### 2. Purchase and payment

You buy the subscription in the Mac App Store or the Microsoft Store. The store charges your store account when you confirm the purchase. The price is the one the store shows on the purchase sheet, and may vary by region and tax. We never see your payment details.

### 3. Automatic renewal and cancellation

- The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then-current price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
- You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your store account settings: in the Mac App Store under App Store › Account › Subscriptions, and in the Microsoft Store under "Services & subscriptions" in your Microsoft account. Pro stays active until the end of the current period after you cancel.
- Refunds follow the store's refund policy; ask Apple or Microsoft.

### 4. When a subscription ends

When a subscription ends, the free plan applies again. Your data is never deleted: projects that need Pro can still be opened, browsed and exported, but not changed until you subscribe again.

### 5. Your data

AnnotaryCat runs on your computer; we do not collect or upload your data (see the Privacy Policy). You are responsible for the data you annotate and process, including having the rights to use it.

The software is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we make no warranty that it is fit for a particular purpose, uninterrupted or error-free, and we are not liable for data loss or indirect damages arising from its use. Keep backups of important data.

### 7. Changes

Changes to these terms ship with app updates and show their effective date. The store notifies you of price changes under its own rules.
