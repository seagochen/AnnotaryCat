### 1. Free plan and Pro

AnnotaryCat is free to use. In the first release, the free plan covers local object detection and image captioning, with no limit on projects. AnnotaryCat Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks this release's image classification, segmentation, ReID dataset curation, local magic wand, remote data sources, the control channel and MCP, and custom fields. The first release is available only on the Mac App Store. Microsoft Store publication is deferred until after the second release, with no date set. Unavailable tasks and future features are not promised as part of this release's subscription. The "AnnotaryCat Pro" page in the app describes what is included.

### 2. Purchase and payment

For the first release, you buy a monthly or yearly subscription in the Mac App Store. Apple charges your store account when you confirm the purchase. The price is the one the store shows on the purchase sheet, and may vary by region and tax. We never see your payment details.

**Free trial.** Each subscription includes a one-week free trial for an account that has not had one in the AnnotaryCat Pro subscription group; the app's AnnotaryCat Pro page shows it only when your account is eligible. You are not charged during the trial. When the trial ends, the subscription starts and the price shown is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial, Pro stays active until the trial ends.

### 3. Automatic renewal and cancellation

- The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then-current price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
- You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your store account settings: in the Mac App Store under App Store › Account › Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the current period after you cancel.
- Refunds follow the store's refund policy; ask Apple.

### 4. When a subscription ends

When a subscription ends, the free plan applies again. Your data is never deleted: projects that need Pro can still be opened, browsed and exported, but not changed until you subscribe again.

### 5. Your data
