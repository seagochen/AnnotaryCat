### 1. Free plan and Pro

AnnotaryCat is free to use. The free plan covers local object detection and image captioning, with no limit on projects. AnnotaryCat Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks this release's image classification, image segmentation, ReID dataset curation, local magic wand, remote data sources (your own SSH server or S3 storage), custom models (run your own training code on this computer or your own servers, keep versions and prelabel with them), the code editor, the control channel and MCP, and custom fields. AnnotaryCat is available on the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store; a subscription bought in one store does not carry over to the other. Unavailable tasks and future features are not promised as part of this release's subscription. The "AnnotaryCat Pro" page in the app describes what is included.

### 2. Purchase and payment

You buy a monthly or yearly subscription in the store you installed the app from:

- **Mac App Store**: Apple charges your Apple account when you confirm the purchase.
- **Microsoft Store**: Microsoft charges the payment method in your Microsoft account. A valid payment method is required to buy.

The price is the one the store shows on the purchase page, and may vary by region and tax. We never see your payment details.

**Free trial.** Each subscription includes a one-week free trial; the app's AnnotaryCat Pro page shows it only when your account is eligible. You are not charged during the trial.

- **Mac App Store**: the trial is for an account that has not had one in the AnnotaryCat Pro subscription group. When the trial ends, the subscription starts and the price shown is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- **Microsoft Store**: each customer can have the trial of each subscription (monthly or yearly) once. When the trial ends, the subscription becomes a paid subscription and your payment method is charged for the first period, unless you cancel before the trial ends.

If you cancel during the trial, Pro stays active until the trial ends.

### 3. Automatic renewal and cancellation

**Mac App Store**

- The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then-current price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
- You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time under App Store › Account › Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the current period after you cancel.

**Microsoft Store**

- The subscription renews automatically for the same period until you cancel it. The next period is charged during the current one.
- You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time at <https://account.microsoft.com/services>, signed in with the Microsoft account you bought it with, and change the payment method there; Manage subscription in the app's Help › AnnotaryCat Pro… opens that page. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the current period and you are not charged again.
- If a renewal payment fails, Microsoft retries it during the current period; if it still fails when the period ends, the subscription is cancelled.
- You cannot switch directly between the monthly and yearly subscriptions: cancel the one you have, and buy the other once it has ended.

### 4. Refunds

- **Mac App Store**: refunds follow Apple's policy; ask Apple.
- **Microsoft Store**: refunds follow the Microsoft Store Terms of Sale and the law where you live; ask Microsoft. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period already charged.

We cannot process store payments or refunds.

### 5. When a subscription ends

When a subscription ends, the free plan applies again. Your data is never deleted: projects that need Pro can still be opened, browsed and exported, but not changed until you subscribe again.
