**Report a problem or suggest a feature:** open an issue at <https://github.com/seagochen/AnnotaryCat/issues>. In the app, Help › Send a problem report… opens that page and makes a diagnostics bundle for you to review and attach. The bundle holds only the version, system information and logs (which record command names, error codes and durations, never project data, file contents or passwords). Once attached, the issue and the bundle are public.

**Subscriptions:**
- Subscribing, renewal and cancellation are handled by the store: in the Mac App Store under App Store › Account › Subscriptions.
- On a new computer or after reinstalling, choose Restore purchases in Help › AnnotaryCat Pro….
- For refunds, ask Apple.

**Common questions:**
- *Where is my data?* In the workspace folder you choose. The app uploads nothing.
- *Why can't the Mac App Store version start training itself?* The store version runs in the system sandbox and cannot start programs outside the app. AnnotaryCat checks the dataset, writes the training configuration and a script, and gives you the command to run in Terminal.
- *Why does the Mac App Store version ask for the workspace again after a reinstall?* Sandbox permissions are tied to the app's signature; after a reinstall or a new version you may need to choose the workspace and dataset folders again.
- *Is my data still there when a subscription ends?* Yes. Projects that need Pro can still be browsed and exported, but not changed until you subscribe again.
